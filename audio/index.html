About Nick

Four decades of experience in audio engineering and dance‑music editing.

Who I Am

Background

Nick’s audio journey began in the early days of home recording and digital editing. He learned the craft through hands‑on experimentation — recording audio from vinyl records and VHS tapes, transferring music between formats, and working with early editing tools that demanded precision and patience. Over the years, he also operated multitrack recording machines and recorded entire performances for dance and gymnastics schools, giving him a deep understanding of how music and movement connect in real‑world settings.

Experience

Over the years, Nick has worked with nearly every format imaginable — recording audio from vinyl records and VHS tapes, transferring music from cassette to CD, and moving projects from CD into fully modern digital editing environments. This broad experience gives him a rare perspective, grounded in both technical mastery and artistic sensitivity.

Dancer’s Perspective

Beyond engineering, Nick is also a dancer. He understands the timing, energy, and pacing that choreographers rely on, and has partnered with studios to complete entire recital seasons.

Why I Do This

Music has been part of my life for over four decades — not just as an engineer, but as a dancer, performer, and lifelong student of rhythm. Editing music isn’t just technical work for me; it’s a way to help dancers feel confident, powerful, and connected to their choreography.

I do this because I know how transformative the right edit can be. A clean cut, a perfectly timed transition, or a custom ending can elevate a performance from good to unforgettable. Helping dancers shine is what keeps me passionate about this craft every single day.

Mission Statement

My mission is to provide clean, impactful, performance‑ready audio edits that elevate your choreography and bring your creative vision to life, always starting from your legally obtained music.

Whether it’s a simple cut, a recital‑ready medley, or a full transformation, I approach every edit with the same care I’ve honed over four decades — blending precision, artistry, and a dancer’s understanding of what makes music move.
