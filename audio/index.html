Four Decades of Audio Expertise

Nick began editing music in the era of analog tape, working with early recording machines long before digital audio workstations existed. He has spent decades transferring and transforming music across formats — from vinyl records to cassette tapes, from cassettes to CDs, and now into the digital world.

Today, Nick brings that deep technical background into modern audio engineering, offering clean, high‑impact edits tailored to dancers, performers, and creators. Every project starts with your legally obtained music. Nick provides the editing skill, technical precision, and creative shaping — you provide the audio.
